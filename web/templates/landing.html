01 ────────

Run your command.

Business Brew checks the resolved package against your company’s published catalog. Approved and available? The native installer takes over.

02 ────────

Get a clear next step.

Need approval or a package that isn’t available? A request is created or reused in your configured ticket system. Follow its tracking link.

03 ✓

Rerun. Get back to work.

Once IT publishes the grant and the package is available, rerun the same command. Business Brew checks again before installation.

↳ Ticket approval and catalog publication are separate steps. Installation is always developer initiated.

YOUR FIRST STEPS
  1. Check with your IT team.They provision your developer credential, managed profile, and installer shims.
  2. Open your usual terminal.Run a supported native command, such as brew install node@22.
  3. Install, or follow the request.If blocked, use the tracking link and rerun after approval is published.
YOUR ROLLOUT, AT A GLANCE
  1. Host the service.Run the Go service on your infrastructure and bootstrap your first local administrator.
  2. Configure and publish.Add exact software targets and grants, connect your request system, then publish the catalog.
  3. Provision your developers.Issue credentials and distribute protected profiles and installer shims through company provisioning.
